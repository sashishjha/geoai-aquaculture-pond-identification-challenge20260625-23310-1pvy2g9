## Hard Rules And Budget Discipline

**Non-negotiable rules (carried from all prior phases):**
1. Never tune the 0.5 threshold. Global monotone logit shifts only, re-derived from real-label OOF t\* after every retrain.
2. Never apply adversarial weights to any loss.
3. Pseudo-rows never enter validation folds; OOF, proxy, and t\* computed on real labels only.
4. One variable per submission.
5. Every submission routes through the numerically safe writer (float64, tie-free, monotonicity assert, CSV round-trip check, positive rate within ±0.05 of π_t).
6. A candidate earns an LB slot only if ΔL > 1σ_L over the best banked configuration on the real-OOF proxy.

**Submission budget: 20 slots (4 days × 5/day). Planned spend is 9–11. The remainder is reserve, not an invitation.**

With 1,030 public rows, each LB score carries sampling noise of roughly ±0.01–0.02. Spending 20 submissions chasing +0.003 deltas is mining noise, and selecting the final pair by public-LB rank after 20 probes is how public-LB overfitting happens. Rules of spend:
- No submission without a passed proxy gate. The LB confirms decisions; it does not make them.
- If two consecutive submissions show proxy-predicted gains that the LB contradicts, STOP — the proxy has drifted; re-validate it against the now-larger LB history before spending another slot.
- Day 4 is selection and confirmation only. No new experiments enter after Day 3 ends.

**Kill criteria (unchanged):** LB AUC moves under a monotone-only change → writer broken, stop calibration work. Predicted positive rate outside π_t ± 0.05 → do not submit.

## Day 1 — Step 9.0: S5 Decomposition And The Hybrid

**9.0a — Decompose S5's F1 gain (zero cost, do first).**
S5 beat S4 on F1 (+0.0043) but lost AUC (−0.0047). Determine whether the F1 gain came from *how many* rows S5 called positive (prevalence effect) or *which* rows (assignment effect):
1. Count positives at the 0.5 cut: n_pos(S4) vs n_pos(S5).
2. Compute the overlap of the two positive sets (Jaccard, and the raw flip counts in each direction).
3. Verdict: if n_pos differs materially and the flipped rows are concentrated near the boundary, the gain is mostly prevalence. If n_pos is nearly equal but the sets differ, it is assignment — the hybrid is impossible; bank S5 and skip 9.0b.

**9.0b — The hybrid candidate (submission #1, if 9.0a says prevalence).**
Construct: S4's score vector (superior ranking) + one global logit shift chosen so that S4's positive *count* equals S5's positive count. This is rule-compliant — the same monotone-shift machinery used everywhere, with the shift constant derived from S5's implied prevalence instead of OOF t\*. It does not touch 0.5.
- Expected upper bound if both components hold: AUC ≈ 0.8263 with F1 ≈ 0.8308 → composite ≈ 0.8290.
- Gate before submitting: on OOF, the same construction (S4-OOF ranking, shift to S5-OOF prevalence) must show ΔL > 0 vs S4-OOF. Then submit as one variable.
- If it wins on LB, it becomes the new conservative anchor, displacing S4.

**Day 1 remainder — Step 9.1: two drift-immune phenology feature families.**
Both are affine-drift-immune by construction — the invariance class that survives a 0.9843 adversarial gap. Neither has been tried (the failed rescue rebuilt level statistics; these are different physical quantities).

**Family A — First-difference / transition dynamics (harvest-cycle signature).** Aquaculture ponds are drained and refilled for harvest: sharp VH spike (exposed bottom), then a drop on refill. Natural water never does this; rice floods smoothly and seasonally. Compute within the observed window, on dB SAR and on MNDWI:
- max and P90 of |Δ month-over-month| for VH and MNDWI
- count of sign changes of ΔVH whose magnitude exceeds a row-scaled threshold (e.g. 0.5 × row IQR of ΔVH)
- count of crossings of the row's own median (VH, MNDWI)
First differences in dB cancel additive gain drift exactly.

**Family B — Within-row cross-band temporal correlation (phenology-coupling signature).** Per row, over observed months: Spearman corr(NDVI_t, MNDWI_t), corr(VH_t, MNDWI_t), corr(EVI_t, NDWI_t). Ponds: flat water signal, flat-low NDVI → near-zero correlation. Vegetation: strong NDVI–water anti-correlation over the growth cycle. Correlation is invariant to any per-band affine drift. Require ≥4 common valid months, else NaN (GBMs route around it). Add water-permanence fraction: share of observed months with MNDWI above the row's own median.

**Admission protocol for 9.1:**
1. Adversarial check first: add features to the masked-train discriminator; every new feature must land in the bottom half of domain importances. Reject any that re-inflate separability.
2. Retrain the S3-config GBM stack with admitted features (one additive block). Gate: ΔL > 1σ_L across ≥3 masking seeds, AUC and F1 components reported separately (flag if one degrades >2σ even when composite passes).
3. If the block passes as a whole, submit (submission #2). If it fails as a block, test Family A and Family B separately (at most one extra submission for the surviving family, #3). If both fail on proxy, spend nothing on LB.
4. Whatever passes becomes the new GBM base config; the monthly index sequences feed the NN regardless of GBM outcome.

## Days 2–3 — Steps 9.2–9.3: The Masked Temporal CNN And The Blend

The four GBMs are one model family in four hats. The NN is the only structural-diversity lever left, and it exploits the one advantage the GBM pipeline can't: on-the-fly augmentation over the masking process itself.

**9.2a — Architecture (dilated 1D CNN, 50k–150k parameters).**
- **Input:** 12 timesteps × C channels. Channels: monthly spectral index sequences (NDVI, NDWI, MNDWI, EVI, AWEI, BSI, NDBI, SAR ratio, VH−VV in dB) + per-row robust-normalized band values + a binary S2-observed mask channel + an in-window mask channel + sin/cos month-of-year encoding. Missing timesteps zero-filled after normalization, always paired with the mask flags. Do NOT feed the 19 dropped absolute features or any raw absolute levels.
- **Body:** 3 conv blocks, kernel 3, dilations 1/2/4, 32→64 channels, GroupNorm (never BatchNorm — unstable at this data size and interacts badly with domain shift), GELU, dropout 0.2–0.3.
- **Pooling — the one non-negotiable detail:** masked global pooling, mean+max computed over VALID months only. This makes the representation window-length invariant. If pooling sees the zero-filled months, the model learns window length as a shortcut and dies on the domain gap.
- **Head:** 2-layer MLP → single sigmoid.

**9.2b — Training protocol.**
- Identical StratifiedGroupKFold folds (block_size=25, no shuffle) as the GBMs — mandatory for legal OOF blending.
- Augmentation on the fly, not static copies: every epoch, every train row draws a fresh window sampled from the empirical test (window_length, start_month) joint distribution, plus fresh 7% S2 dropout. S1 never dropped.
- AdamW, lr 1e-3 cosine decay, weight decay 1e-2, batch 64, ≤100 epochs, early stopping on masked-fold-val AUC, label smoothing 0.05, EMA of weights.
- 3–5 seeds per fold, averaged in logit space — seed variance is real at 1,817 rows.
- No pseudo-labels in NN training. The NN must remain an independent view.

**9.2c — NN admission gates (rank-margin terms, real labels only):**
1. Solo OOF AUC within 0.03 of the GBM blend's OOF AUC.
2. Spearman rank-correlation of NN OOF vs GBM-blend OOF below 0.85.
Pass both → proceed to blend. Fail either → do not blend; keep as a diagnostic and consider one GRU variant (single-layer bidirectional, hidden 64, same masked pooling) before abandoning — but at most one variant. Do not iterate architectures beyond that; it's how days disappear.

**9.3 — Blend and recalibrate.**
- Rank-average the NN into the GBM blend, NN weight swept over 0.15–0.30 on OOF only.
- Map blended ranks through the reference OOF distribution (existing machinery), re-measure t\* on the blended real-label OOF, re-derive the logit shift.
- Gate: ΔL > 1σ_L vs best banked config. Submit (submission #4). Optionally submit the solo-NN-blend at a second weight if the sweep shows two distinct plateaus (submission #5, only if genuinely informative).
- If 9.1 features passed, the NN trains on the updated input set — that is not a one-variable violation because the NN blend is itself the single delta vs the banked GBM-only config.

## Day 3 — Step 9.4: Conditional Extras (Strict Gates)

Only enter this section if Days 1–2 work is banked and ≥2 submission slots remain for Day 3.

**9.4a — Pseudo-labeling Round 2, conditional variant (1 submission max).**
Permitted ONLY if the NN passed its gates. The round-1 failure mode was a self-referential gate; the NN provides the independent voter that fixes it.
- Gate models: 4 GBMs + NN, all trained WITHOUT round-1 pseudo-labels (S5's models are excluded — they've seen their own echoes).
- Thresholds tightened: agreement at >0.98 / <0.02 (was 0.95/0.05).
- Uncertain-row shift kill lowered to 0.05 (was 0.10; round 1 consumed the headroom).
- All other round-1 mechanics retained: prevalence-balanced to π_t, 20% holdout consistency gate at ≥95%, ≤5 diversified copies, fold-safe train-side-only injection, real-OOF non-degradation.
- Expected value is modest; abort without regret if any gate trips.

**9.4b — Backport empirical window sampling to the GBM augmentation (1 submission max).**
The GBMs' 15x augmentation currently draws windows uniformly. Resample the static augmentation from the empirical test (length, start) joint distribution — the same distribution the NN uses. This was roadmap item 7c, never executed. Cheap, principled, single-variable.

**9.4c — Predict-time TTA over S2 dropout (fold into another submission, costs no slot).**
At prediction time, generate ~8 views of each test row with fresh 7% S2 dropout within the observed window (S1 untouched), average in logit space. Pure variance reduction on the S2-dependent features. Validate on OOF first; if OOF shows no gain, skip. This may ride along with 9.4a or 9.4b since it is a prediction-averaging step, not a model change — but if it rides along, declare it in the run log so attribution stays honest.

**Explicitly out of scope for Phase 9:** Optuna sweeps beyond ±20% of current hyperparameters, metric learning, any new loss-level domain adaptation, any threshold logic. If a slot is burning a hole in the budget, leave it unspent.

## Day 4 — Final Selection Protocol

No new experiments. The day has three jobs:

**1. Consolidate the evidence.** Re-run the proxy credibility back-test against the full LB history (now 10+ points). Recompute σ of (LB − proxy-predicted) deltas — this is the real noise scale for the final decision, and it will be larger than the internal σ_L suggests.

**2. Select the final two, maximally different in risk profile — NOT the top two public scores:**
- **Slot A (conservative):** the banked configuration with the most robust construction — fewest moving parts, no pseudo-labels, no LB-fitted constants. As of handoff this is S4 (0.8264) or the 9.0b hybrid if it won. The 1,030-row public LB will reshuffle ±0.01–0.02 on private; the conservative pick is insurance against having fitted the public split.
- **Slot B (aggressive):** the highest proxy-validated configuration from Phase 9 (expected: the NN blend, possibly + phenology features + 9.4 extras if each banked cleanly).
- If the two candidates differ by <0.005 public LB, prefer construction robustness over score: fewer pseudo-labels, fewer aligned constants, fewer LB-informed choices.

**3. Pre-flight both final files.** Full writer assertions, positive-rate check against π_t, and a manual diff of the two files' positive counts and top/bottom 20 rows — confirm the two submissions are genuinely different bets, not the same bet twice.

**Budget note:** if ≥5 slots remain unused on Day 4, that is success, not waste. Resist the urge to spend them — every extra probe biases the final selection toward the public split.

**Log everything** to runs.csv and project_knowledge.md as before: config hash, proxy triple with σ, LB triple, one-line lesson. The next agent inherits your logs, not your memory.
